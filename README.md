# Rimrider

A parody of Rimrunner. It's the same red-rock world, but you ride a motocross bike that morphs into a fast aerobatic glider. Rip across the mesa, hit a jump or a rim, press G, and the bike folds itself into a sailplane in mid-air.

One HTML file, three.js r128 from a CDN, no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder.

## What's different from Rimrunner

- **No tunnels.** The roads stay, but where one used to tunnel through a mountain it now cuts a slot gorge straight through it, some of them hundreds of metres deep.
- **The bike.** It's a motocross bike with a working fork and swingarm, and the rider is strung to the bars and pegs. It leans into turns at the angle a real bike would need. It accelerates hard, tops out around 270 km/h, and falls fast off jumps.
- **The morph (G).** A pod closes over the bike and the rider goes prone inside it. The forks and swingarm fold the wheels up into the belly, long slim wings grow out of the sides, and a red V-tail boom grows out of the back and trails rainbow smoke. Near the ground the pod opens and the wheels drop back down to land on.
- **The glider** flies like an aerobatic plane. The wing is symmetric, so it flies the same upside down. The stick sets a roll, pitch or yaw rate, and the glider holds whatever attitude you leave it in. To take off from the ground, get going, press G, and pull back (Down arrow) to rotate.

## Games

1. **Thermal Hunter**: start high in glider form and stay up on thermals.
2. **Rim to Rim**: ride at a rim flat out, morph as you leave it, glide across and stop on the next pad.
3. **Tumble Drop**: five drops from 300 m, upside down and tumbling. Get it upright and land on the pad.

0. **Free Roam**: no clock, no score. Tab opens a map to teleport anywhere.

Yellow lift beams (+500 ft) and red sink beams (−500 ft) are scattered over the map in new spots on every load.

## Keyboard

W A S D ride, and fly once airborne · G morph bike ↔ glider (C works too) · R F flaps · Y H trim nose down / up (/ neutral) · I throttle with road lock · J K L steer and brake · B rear brake · V views · N map · O tweaks · ; sticky stick · F1 or ` every control.
